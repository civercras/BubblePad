**Free Windows soundboard that plays sounds through your mic.** Mixes your microphone and sound effects into one virtual mic for Discord, games and voice chat.

### Which file do I download?
- **`Bubblepad-Setup.exe` (recommended):** installer. Installs Bubblepad for your user, no admin rights needed, with a Start menu shortcut and an uninstaller.
- **`Bubblepad-windows.zip`:** portable version. Unzip it anywhere and run `Bubblepad.exe`; nothing gets installed.

### What's new in v0.3.0
- **New default look: Chrome.** A silver 2000s media-player chassis with a pixel LCD, fused segmented buttons, LEDs and engraved icons. The bubbly Aqua look is still there as *Classic* in ⚙ → *Look*.
- **Playlist with real waveforms.** Every sound gets its own row with its waveform, length and hotkey; the wave fills up as it plays.
- **Stop button nested into the mic button**, with a red LED while anything is playing.
- **Night mode for Chrome:** graphite chassis with amber screens.

### Since v0.2.0
- **Built-in noise reduction (RNNoise).** Bubblepad cleans your voice before mixing it with your sounds, so you can turn off Discord's noise suppression without losing your effects. Switch it in ⚙ → *Connections* → *Reduce noise* (on by default).
- **Connections panel.** Your mic, the pad, the call and your headphones appear joined by cables that light up with the signal. Click any of them to choose its device.
- **Installer** alongside the portable zip.

### Get started
1. Open Bubblepad. No VB-CABLE yet? It offers to install it in one click (official installer from vb-audio.com → press *Install Driver*), then restart your PC.
2. In Discord or your game, set the microphone to **CABLE Output**.
3. Turn off Discord's noise suppression: Bubblepad already cleans your voice.

### Good to know
- Hotkeys not working inside a game like League of Legends? ⚙ → *Games* → **Restart as administrator**.
- Windows may show a blue SmartScreen warning because the app isn't code-signed yet: *More info → Run anyway*.
- Your sound files are never modified; trims are stored in `config.json`.
