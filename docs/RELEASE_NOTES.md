**Free Windows soundboard that plays sounds through your mic.** Mixes your microphone and sound effects into one virtual mic for Discord, games and voice chat.

### Get started
1. Download **Bubblepad-windows.zip** below, unzip it anywhere and run `Bubblepad.exe`.
2. No VB-CABLE yet? Bubblepad offers to install it in one click (official installer from vb-audio.com → press *Install Driver*), then restart your PC.
3. In Discord or your game, set the microphone to **CABLE Output**.
4. Turn off Discord's noise suppression so your sounds don't get filtered out.

### Good to know
- Hotkeys not working inside a game like League of Legends? ⚙ → *Games* → **Restart as administrator**.
- Windows may show a blue SmartScreen warning because the app isn't code-signed yet: *More info → Run anyway*.
- Your sound files are never modified; trims are stored in `config.json`.
